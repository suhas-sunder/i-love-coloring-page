## Editorial and page-generation policy

One useful original indexable page per approved runtime artwork under `/printables/{primary-category}/{clean-slug}-{stable-id}`. External original images immutable; central resolver, WebP previews, internal SVG, deterministic IDs, approved/blocked manifests. Never commit image corpus, newly generated binaries or review panels. New art requires ownership/licensing clarity, quality and manual-review path. Hold sensitive/anatomically implausible cases until reviewed; don't reject entire classes automatically. Avoid creating many near-identical keyword permutations, doorway pages, thin hubs, or title churn. Weekly package size determined by *reviewability and evidence*, not an asserted Google-safe number. Main indexable page count/canonical changes must be diffed for every package. Never delete/noindex large sections solely for low traffic.
