# Long-Tail Hub Promotion Policy

- Subject minimum: 8 assets
- Combination minimum: 10 assets
- Seasonal and special minimum: 8 assets
- Absolute floor: 6 assets
- Round cap: 128 promoted hubs
- Comparable hub overlap blocker: 90 percent

Only `promote_now` candidates become indexable routes and sitemap entries. Backlog, manual-review, section-only, and rejected candidates stay unrouted.
