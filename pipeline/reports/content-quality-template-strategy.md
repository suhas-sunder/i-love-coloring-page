# Hub Content Template Strategy

This round does not use one universal copy template. It classifies each runtime hub and varies the intro, section headings, examples, and use cases by subject type.

## Broad collection hubs
- Purpose: Help visitors choose a direction without turning the page into a directory.
- User intent: Browsing a large set by subject, style, or season.
- Length: 120-220 words below the gallery
- Avoid: Do not claim every possible subject appears; mention representative patterns from runtime items.

## Animals and nature hubs
- Purpose: Explain the kind of creature or nature artwork users can expect and how to pick detail levels.
- User intent: Printing animal pages for lessons, activities, or relaxed coloring.
- Length: 110-190 words
- Avoid: Do not add animal facts that are not visible in the image set.

## Dog breed hubs
- Purpose: Keep breed wording specific and practical for choosing a page.
- User intent: Finding a printable dog breed page quickly.
- Length: 100-170 words
- Avoid: Do not invent breed traits beyond obvious collection labels.

## Dinosaur and prehistoric hubs
- Purpose: Distinguish species or prehistoric subjects from the broader dinosaur collection.
- User intent: School, prehistoric, and dinosaur-themed printing.
- Length: 110-180 words
- Avoid: Do not use franchise language or unsupported science claims.

## Flowers and plant hubs
- Purpose: Call out plant or flower motifs, simple craft uses, and printable display ideas.
- User intent: Seasonal, classroom, craft, and nature-themed coloring.
- Length: 100-175 words
- Avoid: Do not imply botanical accuracy for every image.

## Food and dessert hubs
- Purpose: Describe the visible treats or food subjects and likely party/classroom uses.
- User intent: Printable food pages for parties, menus, crafts, and light activities.
- Length: 100-170 words
- Avoid: Do not make nutrition, recipe, or commercial-use claims.

## Holiday and seasonal hubs
- Purpose: Support party, classroom, and home planning while keeping dates and themes clear.
- User intent: Finding printable pages for a holiday activity.
- Length: 120-200 words
- Avoid: Do not overpromise complete event packs.

## Fantasy and character hubs
- Purpose: Frame the artwork as generic fantasy, magic, or character themes without protected IP wording.
- User intent: Fantasy-themed coloring and imaginative projects.
- Length: 110-190 words
- Avoid: Do not mention franchise names or imply branded characters.

## Mandala, geometric, and detailed hubs
- Purpose: Help users choose complexity and printing use.
- User intent: Relaxed coloring, detailed pages, and pattern practice.
- Length: 110-180 words
- Avoid: Do not promise therapeutic or medical benefits.

## Kids and easy hubs
- Purpose: Explain simpler page choices and supervision-friendly printing without age overclaims.
- User intent: Quick activities and simpler coloring pages.
- Length: 100-170 words
- Avoid: Do not imply all pages are suitable for every child.

## Vehicles and places hubs
- Purpose: Mention recognizable object or place types from runtime titles.
- User intent: Subject-based classroom, travel, and hobby pages.
- Length: 100-180 words
- Avoid: Do not invent real-world locations unless the titles support them.

## Plushie and cute hubs
- Purpose: Keep the tone calm and practical while describing soft, cute, or kawaii-style subjects.
- User intent: Cute printable pages, party activities, and relaxed browsing.
- Length: 100-175 words
- Avoid: Do not make the page sound childish or cluttered.

## Anti-Boilerplate Rules
- Vary section headings by hub type.
- Use actual representative item titles or section terms in every hub.
- Avoid one universal intro with only the hub title swapped.
- Keep supporting content after the gallery.
- State only PNG, JPG, and WebP as public download formats.
